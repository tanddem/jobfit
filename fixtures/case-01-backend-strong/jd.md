# Senior Backend Engineer

Northwind Payments, Remote (EU)

We are building the ledger and payout services behind a payments platform
that moves EUR 2B a year. You will own services end to end, from design to
production on-call.

## Requirements

- 5+ years of professional backend development
- Strong Python, with production experience in FastAPI or a similar framework
- PostgreSQL schema design and query tuning
- Redis for caching and rate limiting
- Docker and Kubernetes in production
- AWS (ECS or EKS, RDS, S3, IAM)
- CI/CD pipelines and automated testing
- BSc in Computer Science or equivalent experience

## Nice to have

- Kafka or another event streaming platform
- Terraform
- Payments or fintech domain experience
