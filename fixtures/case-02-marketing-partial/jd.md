# Digital Marketing Manager

Brightleaf Skincare, Ho Chi Minh City (hybrid)

We are a fast-growing DTC skincare brand. You will lead a team of four and own
paid, organic and lifecycle channels across Vietnam and Singapore.

## Requirements

- 5+ years in digital marketing, including at least 2 years leading a team
- SEO strategy and execution
- Google Analytics 4 setup and reporting
- Google Ads and Meta Ads campaign management with budget ownership
- HubSpot or a similar platform for marketing automation
- A/B testing of landing pages and creatives
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business or a related field

## Nice to have

- SQL for ad-hoc analysis
- Looker Studio dashboards
- E-commerce or DTC experience
